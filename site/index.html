Five-Layer Delivery Model

Classification, routing, decomposition, execution, and evolution turn a business request into a governed delivery loop. 

Business goal
  -> Classification
  -> Routing
  -> Decomposition
  -> Agent execution and verification
  -> Evidence and evolution
  -> Final business acceptance

Presence Is Not Delivery

Q0-Q1

The capability exists and its parameters, permissions, state, configuration, and dependencies align.

Q2

Backend orchestration, provider coverage, side effects, audit, and authoritative readback close.

Q3

Real business acceptance, reconciliation, required clients, runtime behavior, and release evidence close.

Open Source Boundary

The public repository includes portable methodology assets, rules, skills, templates, audit scripts, and adapter generators. Private strategy notes, raw archives, unredacted cases, process records, and local tool state are outside the open-source scope.
